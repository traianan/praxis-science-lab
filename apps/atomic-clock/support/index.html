Privacy policySupport & testingGoogle Play materials

Praxis Science Lab is the publishing brand used by Traian Anghel. For support or privacy questions, email traiananghel@gmail.com. Include the app name and version. Do not send passwords, medical records, private recordings or other sensitive information.

Report a problem

Tell us your Android version, device model, app version, what you did, what happened and what you expected. Remove personal information from attachments. Never post a tester email list or private feedback in a public repository.

Testing checklist

  • Synchronize on Wi-Fi and mobile data; inspect the server, offset and status.
  • Disable connectivity and verify the offline/last-sync information.
  • Add and remove a time zone, test stopwatch and countdown, restart and check settings.
  • Open privacy information and test a failed custom server and the fallback behavior.

Data removal

Remove saved time-zone entries in the app. Clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall to remove local app records. This build does not explicitly disable Android backup, so device backup or transfer settings may retain or restore app preferences. Remove any device backup separately if needed. Remote server logs are controlled by the time-service operators; clearing app storage does not delete those logs.
