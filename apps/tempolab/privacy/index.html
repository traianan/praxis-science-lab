TempoLab — Privacy policy
Praxis Science Lab · Effective 12 September 2026
Contact: traiananghel@gmail.com

Audio and local storage
TempoLab imports the audio you select through Android's file picker and keeps a decoded copy in its private storage. Tempo, pitch, equalizer, practice sections and voice separation run on your device. Audio files, filenames and separated tracks are not sent to Praxis Science Lab or used for advertising. You choose where to save exported WAV files. A destination you select may be a cloud document provider governed by that provider's policy.

Saved information and deletion
The last imported audio, language, playback settings, named A–B sections and latest separation results are retained locally. A local audio fingerprint associates sections with a recording. Importing another file replaces the current decoded audio. Successful separation replaces previous separated tracks. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling deletes private data; exported files must be deleted separately from their chosen destination. TempoLab has no user accounts or developer-operated server and does not back up its private files through Android backup.

Advertising and internet
The current signed version 1.0.0 has commercial advertising disabled. Google Ads and consent libraries are included but are not initialized for advertising in that build. The development version offers an explicit test-ad preview; opening it connects to Google.
When commercial advertising is configured, TempoLab may show a banner after a completed export. Google may process IP address (including approximate location), device/account identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Required privacy choices are requested before advertising initialization. Where applicable, you can revisit them in Tools > Ad privacy choices. Audio processing works without an internet connection and is not conditional on accepting advertising. No audio is supplied to the ad service.
Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google advertising controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/

Support and audience
If you email us, we receive the message and the contact details you provide and use them to answer your request. Do not send private recordings unless you intend to share them. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. TempoLab is a general music practice tool and is not designed specifically for children. There are no social, messaging, purchase or account features.

Changes
This page will be updated if the app's data practices change. The public policy is at https://traianan.github.io/praxis-science-lab/apps/tempolab/privacy/.
