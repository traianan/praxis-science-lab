Short description

Worldwide internet radio, a globe interface, favorites and recordings.

Full description

Explore internet radio through a modern globe-inspired interface, colorful station cards and three free themes.

Search the Radio Browser directory by country, genre or language. Save favorites, use five presets, add a direct stream URL and import or export your library.

Listen in the background with media controls and station metadata. Adjust the available Android equalizer, set a sleep timer, or record a stream locally as WAV. A Drive view and home-screen widget offer quick access.

All features and themes are free. A planned one-time purchase removes the app banner; it does not remove advertisements broadcast by radio stations. App banners are limited to Discover and Library while playback and recording are stopped. No subscription.

Version 0.1.0 is in testing. Google sample advertising is used in development, and commercial purchasing is not configured.

Data safety worksheet

Review the exact uploaded artifact, provider behavior and active build variants. The development configuration is not a completed Google Play Data safety declaration.

Favorites, presets, history, settings and recordings are stored locally. Import and export use the Android document picker. No microphone is used for stream recording. There is no app account or developer-operated recording upload service.

Internet access is required for the station directory and audio streams. Radio Browser receives directory/search requests and connection information; station servers receive stream requests and the connection IP. Some station URLs use unencrypted HTTP. Foreground playback and notification support screen-off listening.

Google services may process connection information, device identifiers, interactions and diagnostics according to their own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Sample ads do not generate revenue, but can still involve network and device data. Applicable consent and privacy options are handled through Google UMP before requesting ads. Banners are eligible only while playback, buffering and recording are stopped. Google Play Billing is integrated for a planned one-time purchase. Google handles the payment flow; the app verifies and caches purchase information for access restoration. The developer does not receive payment-card details. Production purchase configuration is not active in version 0.1.0.

Media

Capture at least two authentic screenshots from the final app. Do not use simulated screens, synthetic medical fixtures or old-brand captures. Screenshot language must reflect the actual app; English captions do not imply an English-only interface.
