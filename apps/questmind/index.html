QuestMind · Brain Expedition

Explore 100 offline levels with seven brain-teasing puzzle mechanics.

OverviewHelpPrivacySupportRelease preparation

In testing Not yet available on Google Play or Huawei AppGallery. No public APK is offered here.

QuestMind is an offline Android puzzle adventure for ages 13+. Explore 100 levels in five regions with quiz, math, logic, memory, observation, matching-letter connections and untangling challenges. English, Romanian and Spanish, a searchable offline guide, local progress, achievements and a coin shop are included. Android 7.0 or later. Google Play and Huawei AppGallery editions are in development; there is no public store release on this page.

One expedition, seven kinds of challenge

  • Quiz, mathematics and logic sequences
  • Memory and visual observation
  • Connect matching letters without crossings
  • Untangle numbered graph nodes

Progress at your own pace

Explore five regions, earn stars, replay levels, track achievements and use in-game coins. Core gameplay, saved progress and the searchable guide work offline. Website links, optional advertising and store services need a connection.

Development version: 2.2.1+6. Android 7.0+, ages 13+. Commercial store configuration and production signing remain under review.
