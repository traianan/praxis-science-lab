QuestMind Privacy Policy

Local data, optional services and your choices.

OverviewHelpPrivacySupportRelease preparation
English · Română · Español

Scope and publisher

Effective date: 20 October 2026. This policy covers QuestMind development builds, package com.praxissciencelab.questmind, published by PFA Praxis Science Lab, Brăila, Romania. The game is intended for ages 13+ and is in testing. Google Play and Huawei AppGallery commercial releases are not active.

Local gameplay data

Completed levels, best scores, stars, XP, coins, lives and regeneration timestamps, gameplay statistics, achievements, inventory, language and preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. Core play needs no internet, QuestMind account or cloud database. We do not operate a gameplay server or cloud synchronization and cannot remotely access your local save. No separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service is integrated.

Optional advertising

The Google edition includes Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform; the Huawei edition includes Huawei Ads Lite. The current development editions use test advertising identifiers and conservative, non-personalized requests. Test advertising is not a guarantee of zero data processing. Depending on the provider and services invoked, network/IP information, approximate location inferred from IP, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, advertising events and diagnostics may be processed by the advertising provider for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Provider-specific initialization may occur independently of an explicit ad request. Settings offers advertising privacy options; availability and choices depend on the installed edition and provider. Core play works without advertising. Production consent configuration is still under review and this policy does not certify store release readiness.

Store purchases

Google Play Billing or Huawei IAP handles optional purchases when configured. No production products or public commercial payments are activated in this development stage. The store processes account, payment and transaction information under its own terms. QuestMind does not receive payment-card details. Local transaction tokens, validation results and purchase history may be stored to verify delivery, prevent duplicates and restore permanent entitlements. Validation is local; no QuestMind receipt server is used. Purchases do not automatically transfer between stores. Consumed currency is not a cloud backup.

Permissions and external links

QuestMind does not ask for camera, microphone, contacts or precise location. Network access supports optional provider services and vibration supports feedback. Included SDKs can declare their own components and permissions; practices depend on the installed artifact. Opening the website, privacy or support links connects to the browser, website or email service. Ordinary web connection information is handled under the website and provider policies; local game progress is not automatically attached to a support request.

Retention, reset and deletion

Local data remains until you reset applicable progress, clear app storage or uninstall. Reset Progress clears levels, stars, XP, achievements and gameplay statistics while keeping coins, backpack items, permanent unlocks and language. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove all local data and consumables; Android backup is disabled in current builds. Store transaction records remain subject to the store’s retention and legal obligations. Permanent purchases can be restored through the same store when available; locally lost currency may not be recoverable.

Support and your choices

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond and resolve the issue, not for advertising, and retain it only as needed for the request and applicable legal obligations. Our email/hosting providers process information under their own practices. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens, payment credentials or private screenshots. You may request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence, restriction, portability or object where applicable by contacting us. We cannot retrieve or erase a save stored only on your device. You may contact your data protection authority, including ANSPDCP in Romania (https://www.dataprotection.ro/).

Age and updates

QuestMind is designed for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. Do not send personal information about children through support. The public store audience, consent and disclosures must be checked for the exact release artifact before publication. We update the effective date when these practices change.
