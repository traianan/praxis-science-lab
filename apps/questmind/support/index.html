Report a problem

Email contact@praxissciencelab.app with the app version from Settings, phone model, Android version, level number and steps to reproduce. Remove private information from screenshots. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens or payment details.

Common questions

Locked level? Complete the previous level and check region star requirements. Lives regenerate every 10 minutes. For Connect, reorder the right column, join identical letters from left to right and submit only after all pairs are connected with zero crossings.

Read the full EN/RO/ES guide.

Saved progress and purchases

Progress is local, without cloud backup. Avoid clearing app data or uninstalling while troubleshooting. Permanent purchases can be restored through the same store when configured; consumed coins are not recoverable from a QuestMind server. No public commercial products are activated at this stage.

Availability

QuestMind is in development and testing. This page does not enroll testers, collect game data or provide a public APK. Core play does not require a QuestMind account.
