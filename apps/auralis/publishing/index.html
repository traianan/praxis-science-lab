Data safety review

Candidate: local app records are not transmitted to Praxis Science Lab. No advertising, analytics or backend is active. Review the exact signed artifact and voluntary support correspondence before submitting final declarations. AURALIS generates sound locally. Language, appearance, listening preferences, onboarding choices, favorites, saved mixes and session history are stored in the app's local database. We do not receive these records. The app has no user account, advertising, purchases, analytics or application backend. The operating system handles sound output, playback title/state, media controls and audio interruptions. Background playback uses Android's foreground media service and notification or iOS background audio. The app does not record your microphone or request location or phone-state access. The bundled prototype viewer blocks external resources. The Android release does not request Internet access. Remaining checks: - Production signing is not configured; the owner chose to prepare a dedicated AURALIS key later. - Physical Google/Huawei headphone, latency, long-session and power-management checks remain required. - Complete Play Console account, Data safety, foreground-media, health-content, audience/rating and test-track review against the final signed release. - iOS sources are configured, but Xcode compilation, signing and physical iPhone verification have not run.
