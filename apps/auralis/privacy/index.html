This notice describes AURALIS 0.1.0, with advertising and payments disabled. Operator: PFA Praxis Science Lab, Brăila, Romania. Privacy and support: contact@praxissciencelab.app.

Information on your device

AURALIS generates sound locally. Language, appearance, listening preferences, onboarding choices, favorites, saved mixes and session history are stored in the app's local database. We do not receive these records. The app has no user account, advertising, purchases, analytics or application backend.

Device services

The operating system handles sound output, playback title/state, media controls and audio interruptions. Background playback uses Android's foreground media service and notification or iOS background audio. The app does not record your microphone or request location or phone-state access. The bundled prototype viewer blocks external resources. The Android release does not request Internet access.

Keeping and removing data

Local records remain until you remove them, clear app storage or delete the app. On iOS, offloading an app can retain its data; use Delete App to remove it. Android app backup is disabled in this version. System backups on other platforms may retain or restore data under your device settings; manage those backups separately. We cannot remotely inspect or erase a database stored only on your device.

Website and support messages

If you open our website, the hosting provider receives ordinary connection information such as your IP address; the website privacy notice explains this processing. Opening a page does not automatically send your local app records. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Our email provider processes those messages. We use them to answer your request and keep them only as needed for that purpose and applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Support correspondence is not used for advertising or sold.

Security and your choices

Local files use operating-system app storage protections; no additional database-encryption guarantee is made. Protect access to your device. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object where applicable. These rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law. You may also contact your data protection authority, including Romania's ANSPDCP. There is no AURALIS account to delete.

Updates and contact

We update this notice when relevant app behavior changes. Advertising, payments or online services will require a new privacy review before activation. Contact: contact@praxissciencelab.app. Public policy: https://praxissciencelab.app/apps/auralis/privacy/
