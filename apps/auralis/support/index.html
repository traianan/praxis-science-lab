AURALIS — Support

Help, listening guidance and local-data removal.

Testing

  • Launch offline, choose EN/RO/ES, change the theme and restart.
  • Try pure tone adjustment, binaural listening with stereo headphones, favorites and mixes of up to four layers.
  • Save, rename and delete a preset; pause, resume, stop and check local history.
  • Check screen-lock playback, media controls, a phone interruption, headphone unplug and the timer on your own device.
  • Use a comfortable low volume and take breaks. The app limit does not measure acoustic sound pressure.

Data removal

Local records remain until you remove them, clear app storage or delete the app. On iOS, offloading an app can retain its data; use Delete App to remove it. Android app backup is disabled in this version. System backups on other platforms may retain or restore data under your device settings; manage those backups separately. We cannot remotely inspect or erase a database stored only on your device.

Availability

Test-track invitations will be provided separately when a track is configured. This page does not enroll testers or require a review. There is no AURALIS account to delete.

Listening guidance

This app offers sounds for relaxation, focus and general well-being. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment or prevention. Effects may vary from person to person. Do not use at high volume for long periods. If you have hearing conditions, consult a specialist.
