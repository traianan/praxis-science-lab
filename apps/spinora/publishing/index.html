Short description

Create colorful decision wheels, spin your options and save the results.

Full description

A little spin. A clear decision. Spinora helps you choose between everyday options with a customizable decision wheel. Create wheels with 2–50 equally likely options, save them on your device and spin whenever you need a choice.

Start with a template for meals, yes or no, colors, weekend plans, numbers or a break. Customize slice and text colors, bold, italic and underline styles, or add an image from your gallery. Reorder options, paste a list, duplicate a wheel and choose from four themes and three result sounds.

Set a spin duration from 3 to 10 seconds. Keep the latest 200 results in local history, spin again or remove the selected option while keeping at least two options on the wheel. The interface adapts to phones and tablets.

The app opens in English and also supports Romanian and Spanish. Wheels work offline without an account. Optional Google demo ads can be enabled in Settings; they are off by default and do not generate revenue. Commercial advertising is not configured.

Spinora is currently a development version under testing. Google Play availability, final branding and release materials are still being prepared. Published by Praxis Science Lab.

Data safety worksheet

Not finalized for Google Play. Local wheels and images stay on the device, but optional Google advertising may process network, device and advertising information. Review the exact signed artifact, SDK disclosures and production consent flow before submitting Data safety answers.

Wheel names, option text and styling, settings, language and the latest 200 results are stored in app-private storage on your device. Selected gallery images are copied into the app’s private documents directory. There is no Praxis Science Lab account, cloud synchronization or application backend, and the app does not upload your wheels or images to Praxis Science Lab.

Optional Google demo advertising can connect to Google when enabled. A demo identifier does not mean no information is processed by the advertising service.

You select individual images through the platform gallery picker. Internet access supports optional test ads and vibration supports optional haptic feedback. This version includes google_mobile_ads 9.1.0 with Google sample application and banner identifiers. The SDK and its dependencies may process network and device information, identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostics according to Google’s policies.

The test-ad switch is off by default. When enabled, the app initializes the advertising SDK and requests non-personalized demo banners on the main navigation screens. It does not display banners on the wheel or result screen. No production ad identifiers or live monetization are configured. A production consent flow is not yet implemented; non-personalized requests are not a substitute for that flow. Advertising behavior and disclosures must be reviewed before a commercial release.

Media

Capture at least two authentic screenshots from the final app. Do not use simulated screens, synthetic medical fixtures or old-brand captures. Screenshot language must reflect the actual app; English captions do not imply an English-only interface.

Remaining release checks

  • Confirm the final name and permanent package identifier; prepare an upload-key-signed release AAB.
  • Complete physical-device testing of gallery selection, audio, vibration, accessibility and advertising. Automated tests and emulator startup do not cover all these features.
  • Configure production AdMob identifiers and the appropriate consent flow, then reconcile the privacy policy and Play declarations before enabling commercial ads.
  • Add the public privacy-policy link inside the app; the current app displays local privacy information.
  • Prepare final launcher/store artwork and authentic device screenshots from the release build. The website interface previews are not Play Store screenshot assets.
  • Complete the testing and account requirements shown in the actual Play Console account.
