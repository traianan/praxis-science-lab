Short description

Local music, expressive visualizers and a 10-band Pro equalizer.

Full description

A local music player with an original visual identity and detailed sound controls.

Browse songs, albums, artists and folders. Build playlists, manage favorites and reorder the queue. Listen in the background with media-notification and headset controls, playback speed and a sleep timer.

Explore Aurora visuals and a live audio oscilloscope. Pro adds Orbit, Tape and Analog VU styles, a software 10-band equalizer, preamp, balance, stereo width, mono and presets.

Local playback is free. Pro is designed as a permanent, one-time purchase that also removes app advertisements. A discreet banner is limited to the Style screen while playback is paused. There is no subscription.

Version 0.1.0 is in testing. Development advertising uses Google sample IDs only; commercial purchases are not configured.

Data safety worksheet

Review the exact uploaded artifact, provider behavior and active build variants. The development configuration is not a completed Google Play Data safety declaration.

Audio files and embedded cover art are read locally. The library, queue, playlists, favorites, playback position and sound settings stay on the device. The oscilloscope uses decoded playback audio, not the microphone. There is no app account or developer-operated audio upload service.

Media access supports local-library scanning. The Android document picker supports files you select. A foreground playback service and notification allow background listening. Removing a library entry does not delete its original file. Google Play Billing is integrated for a planned one-time purchase. Google handles the payment flow; the app verifies and caches purchase information for access restoration. The developer does not receive payment-card details. Production purchase configuration is not active in version 0.1.0.

Google services may process connection information, device identifiers, interactions and diagnostics according to their own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Sample ads do not generate revenue, but can still involve network and device data. Applicable consent and privacy options are handled through Google UMP before requesting ads. Ads are disabled in the internal Pro evaluation edition and are enabled only in test-ad builds; eligible banners appear in Style while playback is paused.
