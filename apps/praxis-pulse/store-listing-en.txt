Praxis Pulse

Short description
Local music, expressive visualizers and a 10-band Pro equalizer.

Full description
A local music player with an original visual identity and detailed sound controls.

Browse songs, albums, artists and folders. Build playlists, manage favorites and reorder the queue. Listen in the background with media-notification and headset controls, playback speed and a sleep timer.

Explore Aurora visuals and a live audio oscilloscope. Pro adds Orbit, Tape and Analog VU styles, a software 10-band equalizer, preamp, balance, stereo width, mono and presets.

Local playback is free. Pro is designed as a permanent, one-time purchase that also removes app advertisements. A discreet banner is limited to the Style screen while playback is paused. There is no subscription.

Version 0.1.0 is in testing. Development advertising uses Google sample IDs only; commercial purchases are not configured.
