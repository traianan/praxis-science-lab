LuminaPro Privacy Policy

Updated 27 September 2026. App: LuminaPro / LuzPro, package com.praxissciencelab.luminapro, version 1.0.0. Developer: Traian Anghel, publishing as Praxis Science Lab. Contact: traiananghel@gmail.com.

LuminaPro is in development. Commercial advertising identifiers and in-app purchase products are not yet configured. Development versions may request test advertisements; test ads can still transmit information to their provider. This notice describes the Google and Huawei variants separately.

Flashlight and local information

LED control, screen light, battery level and temperature, motion and ambient-light readings, settings, Morse messages and custom light patterns are processed on your device. The app does not capture photographs, video or audio, use location permission, provide its own user account, or upload your messages and patterns to a developer server. There is no separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Camera permission controls the LED only. Notification permission makes active-light controls visible. A foreground service and, when needed for timing, a wake lock keep user-started LED sessions running in the background. Sensors support optional gestures and brightness adjustment. Internet access supports advertising, store purchases and external links. Core lighting functions do not require an internet connection.

Advertising

Banners are limited to Settings while all lighting is stopped. There are no interstitial or video advertisements. The Google variant uses Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform for consent choices. Depending on consent and configuration, Google may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising/device identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, fraud prevention and service operation. Review Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

The Huawei variant uses Huawei Ads Lite and requests non-personalized banners only after an affirmative in-app choice. Non-personalized does not mean that no data is processed: device information, network information, identifiers where applicable, ad interactions and diagnostics may be processed by Huawei. Review the HMS Core privacy information. Advertising choices are available in Settings. Refusing advertisements does not prevent flashlight use. Production consent configuration and regional availability must be completed before commercial launch.

One-time ad removal

The planned non-consumable purchase removes app banners without restricting flashlight functions. Google Play Billing handles purchases in the Google variant; Huawei IAP handles them in the Huawei variant. The stores handle account, payment and transaction information under their policies. We do not receive payment-card details. The app verifies and privately caches signed purchase records for entitlement and restoration. Purchases belong to their respective store accounts; cross-store purchase transfer is not offered. No subscription is planned.

Files, retention and deletion

Settings and patterns remain in private app storage until removed, app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Android automatic backup is disabled. JSON imports and exports use the Android file picker and the destination you select; your chosen storage provider may synchronize exported files under its own terms. Exported files remain after uninstalling and must be deleted separately. Removing app data does not refund purchases or erase records retained by a store or advertising provider.

Support and your choices

Email support receives your address and any information you choose to send. We use correspondence to respond and resolve your request, retain it only as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations, and do not use it for advertising. Do not send passwords or payment credentials. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of correspondence where applicable, and contact your local data-protection authority. For data held by Google or Huawei, use the provider's privacy controls and request channels.

Device permissions can be revoked in Android Settings. Local information is processed to provide the functions you request. Advertising consent, where required, can be managed through the app's advertising privacy controls. Opening this page sends ordinary connection information to GitHub Pages; see the website privacy policy.

Security and changes

Private app data uses Android application storage protections. No additional database-encryption guarantee is made. Protect your device and exported files. We will update this policy when features, providers or data practices change. Store declarations must match the exact released variant and its final configuration.
