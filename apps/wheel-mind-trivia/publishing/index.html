Full description

Spin the wheel and put your general knowledge to the test with Mindo, your cheerful trivia companion.

Explore six categories: Science, History, Geography, Arts, Entertainment and Sports. The question bank contains 1,000 question concepts, each available in English, Romanian and Spanish — 3,000 language versions in total.

Choose your challenge: • Classic: spin the wheel and collect category sigils. • Speed: answer as many questions as you can in 90 seconds. • Survival: start with 15 seconds; correct answers add 4 seconds and wrong answers subtract 5. • Practice: choose a category and play a round of 10 questions. • Daily: take a five-question challenge once a day.

Earn in-game coins, use helpful power-ups and follow your progress in local statistics. Switch between light and dark themes, adjust sound and vibration, and choose reduced motion in Settings.

Questions and core gameplay work offline. No account is required. Progress is saved on your device, without cloud synchronization.

Developed by Traian Anghel. Published by Praxis Science Lab.

Version 1.0.1 adds discreet test banners in Settings only. Gameplay remains available offline and does not require watching ads. Commercial purchases are not configured in this test release.

Data safety worksheet

Not a completed store declaration. Test advertising may transmit identifiers, IP/approximate location, interactions and diagnostics to the store-specific provider. Core gameplay records remain local. Review consent, intended audience, SDK disclosures and observed behavior for each final signed artifact. Do not declare no data collection merely because test ad IDs are used.

Questions, artwork and audio are bundled with the app. Preferences, scores, game statistics, recent questions, coins, lives, daily challenge state and purchase-related status are stored on your device. Core gameplay works offline. There is no developer-operated account, cloud synchronization, gameplay upload, separate analytics or external crash-reporting service. The game does not use camera, microphone, contacts or a location sensor.

Internet and network-state access support test advertising and store services. Banners appear only in Settings. Version 1.0.1 uses test banners only, after applicable advertising choices and when internet is available. Test advertisements do not generate revenue but may still send information to their provider. Refusing ads, no-fill or loss of connectivity does not block normal use. There are no interstitials, app-open ads or mandatory rewarded ads in this configuration.

The Google variant uses Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform. Depending on consent and configuration, Google may process IP addresses and derived approximate location, device/advertising identifiers, interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, service operation and fraud prevention. The Huawei variant uses Huawei Ads Lite and requests non-personalized test banners after explicit acceptance; Huawei may process device/network identifiers, IP addresses, interactions and diagnostics. Non-personalized advertising does not mean no data processing. Advertising choices are available in Settings.

Commercial products and public verification keys are not configured in this evaluation release. Google Play Billing or Huawei IAP handles the optional one-time non-consumable purchase when configured. Stores process account, payment and transaction information under their policies; the developer does not receive payment-card details. Signed purchase records, including store tokens, are cached privately and checked for entitlement and offline access. Online restoration/revalidation contacts the relevant store. Restoring on a new device requires store access; purchases do not automatically transfer between stores. No subscription is introduced.
