Short description

Full description

Spin the wheel and put your general knowledge to the test with Mindo, your cheerful trivia companion.

Explore six categories: Science, History, Geography, Arts, Entertainment and Sports. The question bank contains 1,000 question concepts, each available in English, Romanian and Spanish — 3,000 language versions in total.

Choose your challenge: • Classic: spin the wheel and collect category sigils. • Speed: answer as many questions as you can in 90 seconds. • Survival: start with 15 seconds; correct answers add 4 seconds and wrong answers subtract 5. • Practice: choose a category and play a round of 10 questions. • Daily: take a five-question challenge once a day.

Earn in-game coins, use helpful power-ups and follow your progress in local statistics. Switch between light and dark themes, adjust sound and vibration, and choose reduced motion in Settings.

Questions and core gameplay work offline. No account is required. Progress is saved on your device, without cloud synchronization.

Developed by Traian Anghel. Published by Praxis Science Lab.

Data safety worksheet

Not a completed Play Console declaration. Local gameplay records are not uploaded by the game. Advertising and billing are disabled in the reviewed testing configuration, but store SDKs remain included. Review their manifest components and observed network behavior in the final signed artifact. If services are activated, revise this policy and the store-specific Data safety/consent answers before distribution.

WheelMind Trivia saves language and accessibility preferences, sound settings, scores, game statistics, recently seen questions, coins, lives, daily-challenge state and any Pro entitlement in a local SQLite database. Questions, artwork and audio are bundled with the app. Core gameplay works offline. There is no WheelMind account, cloud synchronization, application backend, analytics service or external crash-reporting service. The game does not upload these local records to Praxis Science Lab.

This notice describes the current testing configuration of version 1.0.0. Store advertising and purchase services are disabled in this configuration. Store-specific libraries are included: the Google build includes Google Mobile Ads/UMP and Google Play Billing; the Huawei build includes Huawei Ads and Huawei IAP. Their presence or declared permissions must not be confused with a guarantee about future releases. The game does not use camera, microphone, contacts or a location sensor.

Advertising or in-app purchases may be enabled in a later release. Before distributing that configuration, we will update this notice to describe the actual providers, data types, purposes, choices and retention, and implement the applicable consent requirements. Purchases would be handled by the selected store; Praxis Science Lab does not receive payment-card details through this game.
