Short description

Track subscriptions, mark payments and plan upcoming recurring costs.

Full description

Keep recurring payments in one place. Add subscriptions manually with an amount, currency, billing cycle and payment date. Organise them using predefined or custom categories and spending groups.

See unpaid amounts due in the next 7 and 30 days, including today. Mark a payment after paying, review payment history and undo a mistaken mark. Local reminders help you follow payment dates and trial endings.

Export CSV or create and restore a JSON backup. Pro tools include a 12-month calendar and projections, monthly budgets, savings scenarios, scheduled price changes, additional reminders and PDF reports. Currencies remain separate, with no external exchange-rate service.

The Standard edition is designed for free use with advertising and an optional Pro subscription. The Lab edition enables Pro tools for evaluation. Version 0.2.1 is in testing; advertising uses Google test IDs and commercial purchases are not configured.

Data safety worksheet

Review the exact release artifact, advertising and billing configuration before completing Google Play declarations. Development testing is not a completed Data safety declaration.

Subscription names, amounts, currencies, categories, spending groups, payment history, budgets, notes and preferences are stored in private local SQLite storage. The app does not connect to your bank, read SMS or email, or upload your financial records to a developer-operated cloud. No app account is required. Android automatic backup is disabled.

Notification permission supports local payment and trial reminders. Scheduling is restored after device restart or time-zone changes. The Android document picker lets you choose backup and export destinations. Manually entered HTTPS links open in your browser.

The development app integrates Google test advertising, consent and Play Billing. Google services may process connection information, device identifiers, interactions and diagnostics according to https://policies.google.com/privacy . Test ads do not generate revenue but may involve network requests. Commercial purchasing and its subscription-verification service are not configured. The developer does not receive payment-card details.
