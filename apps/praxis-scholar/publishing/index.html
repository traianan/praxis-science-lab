Short description

Full description

An offline scientific calculator for study, with an original interface and a two-line expression and result display.

Use SHIFT functions, cursor editing, insert mode, replay, Ans and independent memory. Calculate with powers, roots, logarithms, trigonometric and hyperbolic functions, permutations and combinations.

Work with simple and mixed fractions, percentages, degree-minute-second values, engineering notation and rectangular/polar coordinate conversions. Choose degrees, radians or grads and fixed, scientific or automatic result formatting.

SD mode supports values and frequencies, editing and deletion, sums, mean, population standard deviation and sample standard deviation.

All features are free. Monetization is limited to a discreet banner in Guide, separate from the calculator keypad and display. There is no Pro tier, paid unlock or subscription. Version 0.1.0 is in testing and uses Google sample ads only.

Data safety worksheet

Review the exact uploaded artifact, provider behavior and active build variants. The development configuration is not a completed Google Play Data safety declaration.

Expressions and statistics are processed on the device. Memory, settings and statistical data are saved locally. No app account, microphone, location, contact access or developer-operated calculation service is required.

Internet access is used by the native advertising integration. Calculation content is not sent to a calculation server. Google services may process connection information, device identifiers, interactions and diagnostics according to their own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Sample ads do not generate revenue, but can still involve network and device data. Applicable consent and privacy options are handled through Google UMP before requesting ads. A banner can be displayed only while Guide is open in the foreground. Leaving Guide removes it. Refusing personalized advertising does not restrict calculator functions. No payment SDK is included.
