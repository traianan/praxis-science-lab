Praxis Science Lab is the publishing brand used by Traian Anghel. For support or privacy questions, email traiananghel@gmail.com. Include the app name and version. Do not send passwords, medical records, private recordings or other sensitive information.

Report a problem

Tell us your Android version, device model, app version, what you did, what happened and what you expected. Remove personal information from attachments. Never post a tester email list or private feedback in a public repository.

Testing checklist

  • Capture and import photos with and without location permission or EXIF metadata. Verify originals and frozen capture coordinates/time.
  • Choose or create projects from Camera, after saving and while editing. Check project filtering and selected-photo PDF export.
  • Test automatic and manually added maps online, with cached tiles and without connectivity. Capture must still save when a map is unavailable.
  • Edit stamp text, style and project; remove a map and verify that the original is unchanged.
  • Back up and restore albums, including duplicates and empty projects. Verify the saved ZIP before changing phones.
  • Check English, Romanian and Spanish, large text, camera orientation, denied permissions and GPS wait cancellation on physical devices.

Data removal

Delete a photo in Albums to remove its original, stamped copy and attached map from private storage. Clear Android app storage or uninstall to remove private albums, preferences and map cache; automatic Android backup is disabled. Exported photographs, PDFs and ZIP backups remain at their chosen destinations until you delete them there. Restore adds missing photos and keeps existing IDs. The backup size limit is 2 GB unpacked and 150 MB per file. Map and advertising providers control their own service records and retention.
