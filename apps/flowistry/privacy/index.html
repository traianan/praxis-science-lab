Flowistry Privacy Policy

Privacy information for Flowistry development builds.

Privacy policySupport & testingRelease preparation

Effective date: 16 October 2026. This notice covers Flowistry development builds, package app.praxis.flowistry and the offline test package app.praxis.flowistry.fosstest. The game is in testing and is not publicly released.

Information used by the game

Puzzle attempts, move history, completed levels, local statistics, settings and editor drafts are stored in the app's private storage. Core gameplay works offline. Flowistry does not require a developer account, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. There is no separate developer analytics or crash-reporting integration.

Offline test and store builds

The offline test edition has no Google or Huawei advertising or billing SDK. Store editions include provider-specific services. Commercial advertising and products are not activated during the current development stage. A simulated purchase in a test edition is not a payment or a transferable purchase.

If a test build enables advertising, its provider can process network information, IP address, device or advertising identifiers, interactions and diagnostics. Google builds use Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform; Huawei builds use Huawei Ads and its consent tools. Test ads are not a promise of zero data processing. Applicable choices must be offered before advertising is requested; declining advertising must not prevent core play.

When configured, optional permanent Pro purchases are processed by Google Play Billing or Huawei IAP. The store handles account, payment and transaction information; we do not receive payment-card details. Local purchase records may be used to restore and verify access. Purchases do not automatically transfer between stores. No subscription is offered.

Providers and links

Provider practices depend on the installed edition and enabled services. See Google privacy, Google advertising SDK disclosures and Huawei privacy. Opening this policy or a support link connects to the website or email provider, which has separate infrastructure practices.

Retention, deletion and backup

Local data remains until you clear Flowistry's storage or uninstall it. Puzzle codes shared through another app are handled by the recipient service and may remain there. Earlier evaluation builds allowed Android system backup and device transfer of local data; availability and retention depend on Android and the selected backup provider. Revised builds disable automatic backup so store entitlements are not trusted from a device backup. Clearing game data does not refund a purchase or delete records retained by an app store.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, purchase tokens or private screenshots. Request access, correction or deletion of correspondence using the contact below. We cannot remotely retrieve or erase a puzzle stored only on your device. You may also contact your data protection authority; in Romania, ANSPDCP.

Children and changes

No child-directed advertising configuration or public store audience has been launched. Audience and SDK settings will be reviewed before release. Do not submit personal information about children through support. This page will be updated if the enabled services or data practices change; check the effective date and your installed build.
