Flowistry

Sort colors, plan each pour and explore a calm offline puzzle game.

Privacy policySupport & testingRelease preparation

In testing Not yet available on Google Play or Huawei AppGallery.

Flowistry is an Android color-sorting puzzle in development. Pour matching colors between tubes and plan your moves. The development project includes a 240-level campaign, daily and weekly puzzles, Zen play and a puzzle editor. Mechanics, accessibility and store integrations are undergoing verification.

Core puzzles work offline without a developer account. Android 8.0 or later is required. Google Play and Huawei AppGallery releases are being prepared; no public app download or store release is available from this page.

Plan your next pour

Start with color sorting, then discover locks, target tubes, hidden layers and directed routes. Undo and Restart are part of the core experience. English, Romanian and Spanish resources are included.

Development status

The game is undergoing gameplay, accessibility and distribution improvements. Features and release details may change during testing. Commercial purchases and advertising are not activated.
