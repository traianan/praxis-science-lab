Testing information and support for Flowistry.

Privacy policySupport & testingRelease preparation

Flowistry is in testing

There is no public APK or store installation link. Google Play and Huawei AppGallery editions are being prepared. Android 8.0 or later is required.

Report a problem

Email contact@praxissciencelab.app with Flowistry, your app version, device model, level or mode, and steps to reproduce the issue. Remove personal information from screenshots. Do not send purchase tokens, signing keys or passwords.

Progress and purchases

Progress is stored locally. Avoid clearing app storage while testing saved-game recovery. Test purchases do not charge money and do not establish ownership in another store. A commercial release will provide restoration through the store used for purchase.
