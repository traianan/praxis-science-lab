This website and the Praxis Science Lab publishing activity are operated by PFA Praxis Science Lab. For support or privacy questions, email contact@praxissciencelab.app. Include the app name and version. Do not send passwords, medical records, private recordings or other sensitive information.

Report a problem

Tell us your Android version, device model, app version, what you did, what happened and what you expected. Remove personal information from attachments. Never post a tester email list or private feedback in a public repository.

Testing checklist

  • Open the four home shortcuts, language selector and navigation menu.
  • Scan an accessible test folder and select several artificial images.
  • Move a test selection to local trash, continue selecting, and restore from History.
  • Review exclusions and access limitations; verify that scheduled scans require review.

Data removal

History offers manual removal of old journal records while preserving recoverable or uncertain operation receipts. Local trash has no automatic expiry. Restore needed files before clearing app storage or uninstalling: losing the private journal can prevent restoration of trash payloads. Clearing app data removes preferences and private records but does not necessarily remove shared-storage trash payloads or exported files. Manage exports at their chosen destination. Google controls retention of advertising-service data.
