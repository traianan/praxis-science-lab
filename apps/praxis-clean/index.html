PRAXIS SCIENCE LAB / ANDROID

Praxis Clean

Review storage, select photos in a grid and manage recoverable local trash.

Privacy policySupport & testingGoogle Play materials

In testing Not yet available on Google Play.

A storage and file-review toolkit for Android, currently in testing.

Explore accessible storage, review leftover and unnecessary files, find duplicates, and select multiple photos in a three-, four- or five-column gallery. After moving a selection to local trash, continue with the remaining photos. Review operations and restore eligible files from History.

The toolkit also includes app management, optional cache assistance, image compression to a new copy, exclusions, scheduled scans and contextual battery reports. Android permissions determine what each tool can access. Scheduled scans do not delete files automatically.

Choose English, Romanian or Spanish from the visible language control. A compact, adaptive home screen and a global navigation menu provide access to the tools.

The evaluation build offers an optional Google test-ad check in Settings. The planned model combines advertising with a one-time purchase; commercial purchasing is not configured. There is no Google Play release or public APK download on this page.

Language

English, Romanian and Spanish

Using the app

Android 8 or later. Preview and confirm file operations. Moving files to local trash does not free space and is not a backup. Restore needed files before clearing app data or uninstalling. Permanent deletion and Android cache clearing cannot be undone. Access to private app folders, removable storage and privileged operations varies by Android version and device. Battery reports do not promise additional battery life.
