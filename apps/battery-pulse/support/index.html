Privacy policySupport & testingGoogle Play materials

This website and the Praxis Science Lab publishing activity are operated by PFA Praxis Science Lab. For support or privacy questions, email contact@praxissciencelab.app. Include the app name and version. Do not send passwords, medical records, private recordings or other sensitive information.

Report a problem

Tell us your Android version, device model, app version, what you did, what happened and what you expected. Remove personal information from attachments. Never post a tester email list or private feedback in a public repository.

Testing checklist

  • Check live battery values and charging state against Android.
  • Choose whether to enable local history and inspect gaps and data controls.
  • Configure a widget and check its timestamp and manual refresh.
  • Review the offline privacy notice and the unconfigured purchase message.

Data removal

History → Delete history removes stored samples and reports and pauses collection; alert preferences and the latest widget reading remain. Clearing Android app storage removes all app data, including preferences and the local purchase certificate. Exported files remain at the destination you choose. After verified Pro access is lost, a seven-day grace window permits export of earlier retained readings before Free retention resumes. Restoring after clearing data requires an online purchase check. Retention at a future hosted verification gateway must be documented before activation.
