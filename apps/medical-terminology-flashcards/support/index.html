Help, data-removal information and a practical tester checklist.

Praxis Science Lab is the publishing brand used by Traian Anghel. For support or privacy questions, email traiananghel@gmail.com. Include the app name and version. Do not send passwords, medical records, private recordings or other sensitive information.

Report a problem

Tell us your Android version, device model, app version, what you did, what happened and what you expected. Remove personal information from attachments. Never post a tester email list or private feedback in a public repository.

Testing checklist

  • Use only an approved production content build for store screenshots and release testing.
  • Test offline study, flashcards, quizzes, word building, progress and selective reset.
  • Verify offline pronunciation and behavior when no offline voice exists.
  • Complete TalkBack traversal and any configured consent/ad flows before release.

Data removal

Use in-app reset controls to remove the selected learning records. Settings, ad-frequency state and a storage-recovery copy can remain until all Android app storage is cleared. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private app records. Cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. There is no developer server copy. Device services and any future advertising providers control their own records.
