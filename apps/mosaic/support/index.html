Privacy policySupport & testingGoogle Play materials

This website and the Praxis Science Lab publishing activity are operated by PFA Praxis Science Lab. For support or privacy questions, email contact@praxissciencelab.app. Include the app name and version. Do not send passwords, medical records, private recordings or other sensitive information.

Report a problem

Tell us your Android version, device model, app version, what you did, what happened and what you expected. Remove personal information from attachments. Never post a tester email list or private feedback in a public repository.

Testing checklist

  • Import and export large images, including nine-photo collages; check JPG and PNG.
  • Adjust individual photos, rotate the device and reopen saved projects.
  • Clean unused storage and verify shared project photos remain available.
  • Check EN/RO/ES, small screens, landscape and tablet layouts.
  • Test offline editing, export and app restart; use only demo ads during development.

Data removal

Delete named copies through Projects. Free unused storage removes app-owned photo copies only when no project, draft, recovery file or current undo/redo history references them. Unreadable metadata stops cleanup. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes private projects and settings; Android backup is disabled. Gallery exports remain until deleted separately. Google controls retention of data processed by its advertising services.

Joining a test

Testing invitations and opt-in links will be supplied once a Google Play testing track is available. This page does not enroll you in a test. Report your experience honestly; no positive review is required.
