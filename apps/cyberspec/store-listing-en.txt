CyberSpec: Sensor Analyzer

Short description
Explore live sensors, Wi-Fi signals, battery health and sound spectra.

Full description
Turn your Android device into a pocket diagnostics lab with a dark, neon dashboard. CyberSpec reads measurements directly from Android, with no account or developer-operated backend.

Explore accelerometer, gyroscope and magnetometer readings with live X/Y/Z plots. Freeze a trace, inspect sensor details and use the digital bubble level with relative zero.

Monitor battery temperature, voltage, current and health, alongside RAM, storage and Android thermal status. Availability depends on the device.

Inspect Wi-Fi signal strength, link speed, band and channel. Compare nearby networks and use audible signal-finder feedback while moving around. Scanning follows Android permissions and scan limits.

Start the microphone manually to view a waveform, dBFS level and FFT frequency spectrum. An adjustable calibration offset provides an approximate sound-pressure estimate.

Create and export local JSON diagnostic reports through the Android file picker. English is the default; Romanian and Spanish are included.

Version 0.1.0 is in testing. The offline edition contains no advertising SDK or Internet permission. A separate AdMob TEST edition demonstrates Google sample banners and interstitials; ads require Internet while diagnostic tools remain local. Commercial advertising is not configured.
