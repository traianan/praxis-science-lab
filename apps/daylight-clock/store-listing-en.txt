Daylight Clock

Short description
Personal clock widgets with sunrise, sunset and daylight at a glance.

Full description
A customizable clock and daylight companion for your Android home screen.

Choose a city from the offline catalog, enter coordinates and a time zone, or request your device location. See sunrise, sunset, civil twilight, day length and the change since yesterday, calculated locally without a solar web service.

Personalize three resizable widget layouts with 12/24-hour time, six fonts, date formats, separate colors, RGB/HEX controls, transparency and two-color gradients. Show the solar arc, a daylight bar or just the clock. Each widget keeps its own place and style.

Choose tap actions for the clock and date, open your phone alarms or calendar, and explore another time in the app preview.

All features are free. The development build may show Google test banners only in Style, after the consent flow. A one-time Remove Ads purchase is prepared but inactive until configured. No subscription. Version 0.3.0 is in testing.
