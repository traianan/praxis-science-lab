Website Privacy

Privacy information for the Praxis Science Lab documentation website.

Effective date: 2026-11-06. This notice covers the public Praxis Science Lab website.

Who operates this website

The website is owned and operated by PFA Praxis Science Lab, a Romanian sole proprietor (PFA), at Brăila, Str. Ion C. Brătianu, nr. 1, postal code 810067, Romania. Contact for privacy and support: contact@praxissciencelab.app.

Website operation and browser storage

This static public website has no visitor account, contact form, advertising or analytics script added by Praxis Science Lab. It stores your selected color theme in browser local storage to remember your preference. You can remove this setting using your browser’s site-data controls. We do not add advertising or analytics cookies to this public site.

The main domain is hosted by Vercel. Delivering and securing pages involves ordinary technical connection information, such as IP address and request information. See Vercel’s privacy notice. Legacy website addresses redirect through GitHub Pages, whose hosting is subject to GitHub’s privacy information. Hosting providers may process information outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards described in their policies.

Email and support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you provide. We use correspondence to answer requests and resolve issues. Depending on the request, the basis is our legitimate interest in responding and maintaining services, steps requested before a contract or performance of a contract, or a legal obligation. We do not use support correspondence for advertising or sell it.

Access is limited to what is needed to handle your request and operate the supporting email/hosting services. Correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations; retention depends on the request and any continuing dispute or obligation. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, medical records or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of applicable personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests, by contacting contact@praxissciencelab.app. Rights depend on the applicable legal conditions; we may need to verify a request. You may also complain to the Romanian supervisory authority, ANSPDCP.

Apps, private administration and external links

App-specific privacy policies describe the information used by each app. This notice concerns the public presentation website; private PFA administration has separate authenticated access and is not a public visitor service. External websites and stores have their own policies. GitHub issues are public; do not post private information there.

Updates

The effective date changes when this notice is revised. For contact details, see Contact.
